Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

diato draft uploader is a personal utility for uploading authorized music-promotion videos to TikTok as drafts.

Data handled

The utility may process TikTok authorization data, basic TikTok profile information, and video files selected by the authorized user. This data is used only to authenticate the user and upload requested drafts.

Storage and sharing

The utility does not sell personal data. Data is not shared with third parties except TikTok as required to provide the requested draft-upload function. Authorization credentials should be stored locally and can be revoked through TikTok.

Contact

For questions, use the repository at github.com/cx2rcjymc6-lang/tiktok-oauth-callback.
